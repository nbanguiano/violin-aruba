I’ve been sharing that passion through live performances for more than 65 years; since I was 5 years old! Here in Aruba, I play for weddings, proposals, celebrations, and live music venues around the city, with the sea and the island as our backdrop.

A few things you might be wondering

Before we
make music.

Can you play our special song?

I play classical pieces and contemporary songs, and can personalise the repertoire around your celebration. Share the song you have in mind when you enquire so we can discuss it.

Do you play for more than weddings?

Yes. Proposals, private parties, christenings, bar mitzvahs, restaurant evenings and other special events are all part of my work.

We’re planning from abroad. Where do we start?

Send me your date, the occasion and your venue if you have one. Add a little about the music and atmosphere you’re imagining. We can start the conversation by email or WhatsApp.

How much does a performance cost?

Get in touch with your event date, location and the part of the day you’d like music for. I’ll discuss availability and a quote for your event with you.

Where can we see you play on the island?

You can find me at Azzurro Ristorante in Playa Linda Beach Resort on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday, 7:00–9:30 pm, and at Gianni’s Restaurant on Saturday, 7:30–9:30 pm. See the live schedule.
